DRAÍOCHT

A small window into the year.

The whole sunset-to-sunset day containing a new moon is day 1. The date advances at sunset in Orlando. The vernal equinox changes the year without resetting the lunar date.

The ring shows each day of the current lunar month. Its small Moon drawings represent the midpoint of each sunset day; the central Moon follows the displayed time. Use the arrows, date picker, or ring to explore. Choosing a Gregorian date opens noon in Orlando. Now returns to the live clock.

The lower-left corner

Try the next seasonal marker, the current Natural Tuning reflection, or the next full moon. A marker is a reference point; reflection and celebration have room around it.

What is calculated

Dates use published US Naval Observatory moon phases and Orlando sunsets, and NAOJ solar markers. Times are published to a minute. Sunsets assume a level horizon and standard refraction.

The central Moon uses lunar surface imagery from NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio, assembled from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data. Its lighting follows the phase interpolated between published new, quarter, and full moons. This is a rendering with approximate lighting, shown with lunar north up; it does not reproduce local sky tilt, libration, or eclipses. The small symbols around the ring are schematic.
